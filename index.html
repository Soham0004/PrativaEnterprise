About Us

A Legacy of Excellence and Expertise

For over five years, Prativa Enterprise has stood as a cornerstone of quality construction in Kolkata. While the Prativa name represents our modern era of innovation, our foundational expertise spans more than two decades. Having operated under various successful banners for over 20 years, our leadership brings a lifetime of seasoned craftsmanship and industry knowledge to every site we manage.

We understand that any construction project is a significant investment. That is why we uphold a legacy of transparent communication, meticulous project management, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Whether we are crafting a private family residence or developing a large-scale commercial complex, we handle every project with the deep professionalism that only twenty years of experience can provide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got doubts? We believe in 100% transparency.

What types of projects do you handle?

We handle a wide range of projects, including new residential construction, commercial buildings, complete home renovations, interior design, and structural repairs. No project is too big or small.

Are you a licensed and insured company?

Yes, absolutely. Prativa Enterprise is a fully licensed and insured general contractor in Kolkata. We adhere to all local building codes and safety standards to ensure your project is secure and compliant.

How is the project cost determined?

We provide a detailed, transparent quote after an initial consultation and site assessment. The cost depends on the project scope, materials selected, labor involved, and required permits. We ensure there are no hidden charges.

How do you ensure the project stays on schedule?

We use dedicated project management to create a realistic timeline. A dedicated project manager will provide you with regular updates and ensure all milestones are met, coordinating between our teams and suppliers to prevent delays.
